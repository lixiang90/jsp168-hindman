Reviewed chain
1. The original product native bracket really is coordinatewise, by its
   previously proved Lie algebra equivalence. The actual smooth inclusion
   has identity differential equal to the true relative model inclusion.
   Its adjoint differential therefore intertwines the actual brackets.
2. Relative Lie layer i is Fi x Fi with first-minus-second in Fi+1.
   Expanding B(v1,w1)-B(v2,w2) as
   B(v1-v2,w1)+B(v2,w1-w2) gives the extra next layer. This
   works also at layer zero; no informal step decrease is used.
3. Constant product corrections use F1=top, hence have weight one with
   arbitrary coefficient size. Weighted Picard perturbation is an exact
   product-log difference, not an assumption that BCH preserves weights.
4. The shift difference and three actual constant perturbations telescope
   to the ORIGINAL first-minus-second logarithmic polynomial. Applying
   scalar duals to all real assignments proves aggregated coefficient
   equality, retaining cancellations. Together with the previously proved
   actual relative coefficient membership this gives genuine term weights.
5. Generic iterated native group differences receive actual antitone,
   bracket and exponential-layer proofs. They prove filteredness of the
   CONSTRUCTED relative polynomial. Integer restriction uses the real
   additive map n -> (fun _ => (n : real)); it keeps the exact original
   normalized shifted pair, including negative shifts and samples.
6. Projection to the terminal diagonal quotient preserves those exact
   samples and all iterated differences. The actual image filtration
   vanishes at s*d, so all such length-s*d differences are identity.
   The reduced group retains the native step bound s; it is its actual
   filtration degree, not necessarily its nilpotency class, that decreases.
7. Actual reduced Lie layers are images under the true model quotient
   projection. Native Log membership is proved in both directions using
   actual inclusion/projection Exp compatibility. Smooth projection and
   its proved differential give the true image bracket condition.
8. Exp/Log identify each actual reduced group layer with its finite
   dimensional projected real subspace. Closedness and contractibility
   follow with the actual subtype topology; connectedness and simple
   connectedness follow. No image of a general closed subgroup is assumed
   closed, and no lattice rationality follows just from this topology.
9. The constructed reduced polynomial has these exact projected Lie
   weights. Coefficients at degrees >= s*d vanish. Zero-vector-term
   removal preserves evaluations and gives ordinary degree <= s*d-1.
   This bound also handles s*d=0 via truncated natural subtraction and
   the terminal zero Lie layer; it does not assume positive s.
