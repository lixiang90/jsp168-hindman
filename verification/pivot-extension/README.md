# Original full-raw pivot extension

`selected_alignment_finite_scale_pivot_extension` proves the actual local
extension used in the uniform Alignment iteration. Its inputs are the
original `MasterParameterConditions`, selected padding/principal embedding,
actual represented prediction rows, positive threshold, and the fixed finite
plan's own-target, clearing-denominator and catalogue-scale conditions.

For every earlier event `E`, the theorem produces one error tending to zero
and the uniform eventual inequality

`P(E and some original route secures every requested threshold)
   >= P(E)/(3 Q^2) - error`,

where `Q` is the cardinality of the plan's route set. The event on the right
of the inequality is evaluated on the SAME original full raw sample after
translation removal. The error may depend on the fixed models and threshold;
the multiplier depends only on the finite plan.

## Proved assembly

- Exact disintegration of the full raw product into the genuine outside
  coordinates and original signed harmonic pivot kernel.
- The actual local route mass is invariant under equal earlier data and
  equal microcell/residue exposure, including degenerate empty windows.
- Incomplete selected padding cells have vanishing mass under the original
  principal cutoff law. No master conditions for the modified gap view are
  used.
- The previously proved local uniform failure limit and endpoint discard
  imply vanishing exceptions under the exact fine conditional joint law.
- The original uniform-to-harmonic likelihood estimate is applied once to
  the union of successful routes. It supplies `1/(2 Q)` coverage on good
  fine fibers; the actual outside Markov argument supplies `1/(3 Q)`.
- Every fixed route shift is divisible by the primorial and bounded by a
  fixed constant times the original auxiliary scale squared, for all raw
  anchors. The harmonic translation estimate removes these shifts uniformly
  over every earlier event, losing only an error tending to zero.
- Secured predicates are defined first for all signed pivots. Only values
  on the original harmonic support are converted to natural coordinates,
  so no additional shifted-pivot positivity assumption is needed by the
  extension endpoint.
- The final predicate is the existing `principalNativeScaleObservation`
  threshold predicate. Every scale lookup is justified by actual catalogue
  membership and every model by `NativePieceRepresented`.
